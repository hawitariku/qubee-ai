🔒 Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and protect your information when using Qubeessaa AI.

Last updated: January 2026

Overview

Qubeessaa AI is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard information when you use our free Afaan Oromo spell checker service.

Key Point: We do not store or permanently retain the text you submit for spell checking. Your text is processed temporarily to generate corrections and then discarded.

Information We Collect

1. Text You Submit for Spell Checking

When you use our spell checker, you submit text in the Afaan Oromo language. This text is:

  • Processed temporarily by our AI system to generate spelling and grammar corrections
  • Not stored permanently on our servers
  • Not shared with third parties
  • Automatically discarded after processing is complete

2. User Feedback Data

When you click the ✓ (accept) or ✗ (reject) buttons on corrections, we collect:

  • The original word that was flagged
  • The suggested correction
  • Whether you accepted or rejected the suggestion
  • Timestamp of the feedback

This feedback data is anonymized and used solely to improve the accuracy of our spell checker for all users.

3. Technical Information

Like most websites, we automatically collect certain technical information:

  • IP address (for rate limiting and security purposes)
  • Browser type and version
  • Device type (mobile, tablet, desktop)
  • Pages visited and time spent on the site
  • Referring website (if you came from another site)

How We Use Your Information

Information Type Purpose Retention Period
Text for spell checking Generate corrections and suggestions Processed immediately, not stored
User feedback (✓/✗) Improve spell checker accuracy Stored anonymously, indefinitely
IP address Rate limiting, security, analytics 30 days maximum
Browser/device info Optimize user experience 30 days maximum

Third-Party Services

Google AdSense

We use Google AdSense to display advertisements on our website. Google AdSense may:

  • Use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to our website or other websites
  • Collect information about your browsing behavior for ad targeting
  • Store and access information on your device

You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings.

Note: Google AdSense operates under Google's Privacy Policy. We do not control Google's data collection practices. Please review Google's Privacy Policy for more information.

Web Hosting and Infrastructure

Our website is hosted on Railway.app. Railway may collect technical information such as server logs, IP addresses, and performance metrics as part of their hosting services. This information is governed by Railway's privacy policy.

Cookies and Local Storage

We use cookies and browser local storage for the following purposes:

  • Welcome popup preferences: Remember if you've seen the welcome guide
  • Dark mode setting: Remember your light/dark theme preference
  • Guide completion status: Track which interactive guides you've completed
  • Google AdSense: Advertising cookies (managed by Google)

You can disable cookies in your browser settings, but this may affect some functionality of the website.

Data Security

We implement appropriate security measures to protect your information:

  • HTTPS encryption for all data transmission
  • Rate limiting to prevent abuse
  • Regular security updates and monitoring
  • Minimal data collection and retention

Important: While we take security seriously, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. Do not submit highly sensitive or confidential information through our spell checker.

Your Rights

You have the following rights regarding your information:

  • Access: Request information about what data we have collected
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your information (where applicable)
  • Opt-out: Opt out of data collection by not using the service

Children's Privacy

Our service is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can delete such information.

International Users

Our service is primarily intended for users of the Afaan Oromo language, many of whom are located in Ethiopia and Kenya. If you are accessing our service from outside these regions, please be aware that your information may be transferred to and processed in countries with different privacy laws.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make changes, we will:

  • Update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page
  • Notify users of significant changes through the website
  • Continue to protect your information in accordance with this policy
