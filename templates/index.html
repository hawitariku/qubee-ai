📚 About the Afaan Oromo Language

🌍 Language Overview

Afaan Oromo (also called Oromiffa) is the most widely spoken Cushitic language in the world, with over 40 million native speakers. It is primarily spoken in Ethiopia and Kenya, making it one of Africa's major languages.

🔤 Writing System

Afaan Oromo uses the Qubee alphabet, a Latin-based writing system officially adopted in 1991. This system uses 26 letters plus special combinations to represent the unique sounds of the language.

✍️ Common Spelling Patterns in Afaan Oromo

Long Vowels (Sagalee Dheeraa)

Long vowels are written by doubling the letter:

  • ee - as in "deeme" (went)
  • oo - as in "boo'aa" (cry)
  • uu - as in "dhuguu" (to drink)
Special Sounds

Unique letter combinations (digraphs):

  • dh - retroflex sound in "dhuguu"
  • ch - as in "chakkaa"
  • ny - as in "nyaata" (food)
  • ph - as in "phaaroo"
  • ' - glottal stop (hudhaa)
